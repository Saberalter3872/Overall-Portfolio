Yeo See Siang wrote that "There is no real meaning to life. But... that is exactly why the meaning we find is beautiful beyond all measure. May all beauty be blessed"


I am a Mechanical Engineering Undergraduate at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. At the same time, I work as a photographer on the side.


I believe in the power of records and the memories they create. Using photos, there is meaning to documenting experiences between people, nature and the wider world. They bring us closer to others across the globe and connect cultures, unravelling the mysteries and unspoken prejudices we have. With it, we can break down barriers and bring humanity together as one. As such, I thrive on visiting, learning and trying different cultures and cuisines. Growing up in Singapore with 4 different national languages, I grew to understand how learning the language of others is in itself a type of respect and show of goodwill that you are trying to assimilate, not invade, one's place of belonging. This skill has allowed me to stay cool headed and navigate the ins and outs of workplace diplomacy whether in work or in clubs during school. It has also allowed me to engage with clients in my photography work that come from different backgrounds who are not used to the Singaporean way of life. Those moments taught me how to hold my composure, observe quietly, and bring real empathy and analytical rigor to collaborative teams. I am driven by the belief that the best technology is built not just for abstract users, but for living, breathing human beings.
